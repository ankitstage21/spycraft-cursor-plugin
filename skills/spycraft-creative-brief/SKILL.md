# Creative brief

1. Establish the user's product, audience, offer, brand voice, channel, objective,
   allowed claims, and proof assets. Label missing product facts rather than inventing them.
2. Check connection. Use `list_boards`, `get_board`, or `list_saved_ads` when the user
   refers to saved inspiration. Otherwise resolve relevant competitors and retrieve a small
   evidence set with competitor research tools.
3. Extract reusable principles: hook structure, pacing, visual proof, objections, and offer
   framing. Keep references linked. Competitor scripts and imagery are research material;
   draft original executions and do not claim ownership of those assets.
4. Produce three distinct concepts. For each specify audience insight, original hook,
   scene sequence, editable script, proof needed, CTA, and test hypothesis. Separate facts
   about the user's product from hypotheses and placeholders.
5. Add a measurement plan with a primary metric, comparison design, and stopping
   criteria appropriate to available traffic; never invent expected uplift.
6. Deliver drafts. Do not submit production, uploads, tracking changes, paid jobs, or publishing
   merely because the user requested a brief. Existing explicit authorization may cover follow-up work.

Deliver an evidence section and three executable briefs with claims to verify and assets needed.
